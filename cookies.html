Cookie Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. What this page covers

This page lists the cookies that hannune.ai actually sets and how you can accept or reject them. Our full Privacy Policy covers how we handle data more broadly, for both hannune.ai and the Entity Resolution API console.

2. Cookies we use

CookieSet byPurposeDuration
_ga, _ga_* Google Analytics (GA4) Counts visits and distinguishes users so we can see how many people read this site. Only set after you accept analytics cookies below. Up to 14 months

We also run our own page-view counter (a small script loaded from www.hannune.tools). It sets no cookies: it sends the page you are on and a one-way hash of your IP address, not the address itself, and it runs regardless of your choice below because it is not a cookie.

We do not set advertising cookies, and we do not use cookies to build profiles for ad targeting. The thumbnail images on this site that link to YouTube videos are plain images (served from img.youtube.com); they do not embed a YouTube player and do not set YouTube cookies.

3. Your choice

hannune.ai does not load the Google Analytics script at all until you accept it in the banner shown on your first visit. If you reject it, no analytics cookie is set and no analytics script runs.

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics across all sites with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

5. Changes to this policy

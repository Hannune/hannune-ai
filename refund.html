This Refund Policy describes when and how you may receive a refund for services purchased from Hannune (한누네). It applies to all Hannune customers worldwide and supplements the consumer protection rights that may apply under your local law.

1. Entity Resolution API subscriptions

1.1 First subscription — 14-day guarantee

Full refund available for your first ER API subscription within 14 days of the initial payment, for any reason. This satisfies the EU Consumer Rights Directive and exceeds most industry standards. No explanation required.

1.2 Subsequent billing periods

After the initial 14-day window, subscriptions are billed monthly and non-refundable for billing periods already started. You may cancel at any time to prevent future charges; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you retain access to the service until then.

1.3 Overage charges

Overage charges ($0.001 per additional match beyond your monthly quota) are billed at the start of the following billing cycle and are non-refundable. To avoid unexpected overage, you can set a hard cap via your account dashboard or email us to configure one.

1.4 Annual plans (if applicable)

If annual pricing is introduced, prorated refunds will be available within the first 14 days of the annual term. After 14 days, annual plans are non-refundable for the remainder of the term, though the discount applied remains honored.

1.5 Service outages

Business tier customers with a 99.9% uptime SLA are eligible for service credits in the event of verified downtime exceeding the SLA threshold. Service credits are applied to future billing periods and cannot be exchanged for cash.

2. Consulting engagements

2.1 Deposit and milestone payments

Consulting engagements begin with a 30% deposit upon signature of the Statement of Work (SOW), with the remaining balance invoiced at agreed milestones.

2.2 Cooling-off period

You may cancel a signed SOW within 7 calendar days of signature for a full refund of the deposit, provided project work has not yet begun. Once kickoff has commenced (data sharing, onboarding calls, or written work product delivered), this option no longer applies.

2.3 Early termination

Either party may terminate a consulting engagement for material breach, insolvency, or force majeure. If Hannune terminates due to breach by the Customer, fees paid are non-refundable and any work-in-progress is billable up to the termination date.

If the Customer terminates without cause after kickoff, refunds are calculated as follows:

  • Before 25% completion: 50% of fees paid, less delivered work product.
  • 25–75% completion: 25% of fees paid, less delivered work product.
  • After 75% completion: no refund; all paid fees are retained and remaining work product is delivered.

Completion percentages are measured against the deliverable schedule in your SOW.

2.4 Starter PoC to Standard Production credit

If you complete a Starter PoC engagement and sign a Standard Production SOW within 60 days, 50% of the Starter PoC fee ($4,500) is credited toward the Standard Production engagement. This credit is not redeemable for cash.

2.5 Delivery guarantees

The Standard Production package includes a 6-week delivery guarantee. If we fail to deliver the defined scope by the agreed deadline due to reasons within our control, you are entitled to a 20% refund of the package fee. This does not cover delays caused by Customer dependencies (e.g., delayed data access, approval bottlenecks).

3. Ops Retainer subscriptions

The Ops Retainer requires a minimum 3-month commitment. After the initial term, it renews monthly and can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice. Fees already paid for the current month are non-refundable; however, any pre-purchased hours remain available through the end of the notice period.

4. How to request a refund

  1. Email us. Send your refund request to support@hannune.ai with your account email, order/invoice number, and the reason for the refund (reason is optional for the 14-day guarantee).

  2. We acknowledge within 2 business days and confirm eligibility. For ER API refunds, we may ask Paddle to initiate the refund on our behalf.

  3. Refund is processed within 5–10 business days after approval. Payment card refunds typically appear in 3–5 additional business days depending on your bank; bank transfer refunds for consulting may take up to 10 business days.

  4. You receive confirmation by email once the refund is issued.

Payment method. Refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. For ER API subscriptions, this means Paddle will refund your card, PayPal, or other method used at checkout. We cannot issue refunds to a different card or account.

5. What is not refundable

The following are non-refundable in all circumstances:

  • Overage charges on the ER API
  • Subscription fees beyond the initial 14-day guarantee window
  • Consulting work already delivered and accepted
  • Custom development completed under a signed SOW
  • Third-party costs passed through at cost (e.g., cloud infrastructure, LLM API usage)
  • Waitlist-related promotional discounts after the discounted period has been consumed

6. Chargebacks and disputes

If you have a concern about a charge, please contact us first at support@hannune.ai. We aim to resolve all billing issues amicably. Initiating a chargeback without first contacting us may result in suspension of your account and may be considered a breach of our Terms of Service.

For ER API subscriptions processed by Paddle, Paddle's dispute resolution procedures apply in addition to these terms. See Paddle's Buyer Terms (paddle.com/legal/buyers-terms) for details.

7. EU consumer rights

If you are a consumer resident in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have additional rights under the Consumer Rights Directive, including a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal for most digital purchases. Our 14-day money-back guarantee meets or exceeds these requirements.

Note that under Article 16(m) of the Consumer Rights Directive, the right of withdrawal may be lost once a service has been fully performed with your express prior consent. By using the ER API during the 14-day period, you acknowledge this, though our policy is to honor refund requests regardless.

8. Korean consumers

Customers who are consumers resident in the Republic of Korea benefit from the protections of the Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (전자상거래 등에서의 소비자보호에 관한 법률), including the right to cancel digital content purchases under the conditions specified therein.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified to registered users by email at least 30 days before taking effect. The policy version in effect at the time of your purchase governs that purchase.

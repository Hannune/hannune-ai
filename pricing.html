Common questions

Anything we haven't covered? Email contact@hannune.ai and we'll respond within 2 business days.

Is the consulting really available now?

Yes. Consulting is our active revenue line with delivered case studies — most recently LG Energy Solution (multi-agent talent pipeline) and Gahyun Tax Accounting (Korean tax search). New engagements kick off within 2 weeks of contract signature.

When does the ER API launch?

Private beta is scheduled for Q2 2026. Waitlist members get access 2 weeks before public launch, 30% off their first 3 months, and the ability to lock in these prices permanently.

What's included in the fixed consulting prices?

Pricing assumes data in standard formats (JSON, CSV, Postgres, common PDF structures) and access to typical cloud environments. For highly unstructured data, legacy system migrations, regulated industry compliance, or custom integration work with proprietary systems, we scope these separately during the discovery call.

Who processes payments?

Once the ER API launches, Paddle.com will act as the Merchant of Record for all subscriptions. They handle global tax compliance (VAT, GST, sales tax), PCI compliance, and refund processing. Consulting invoices are paid directly via bank transfer.

What's your refund policy?

14-day money-back guarantee on your first ER API subscription, no questions asked. Overage charges are non-refundable. Consulting packages follow the terms in your signed Statement of Work. Full policy at /refund.

Can we start with Starter PoC and upgrade?

That's the recommended path. Starter PoC findings credit 50% toward a Standard Production engagement if we move forward within 60 days. Most clients start here to validate fit before committing to the 6-week build.

Do you work with data in Korea specifically?

Yes — Korean-language entity extraction, mixed Korean/English documents, and Korean business registries are a core strength. Recent projects include LG Energy Solution and Gahyun Tax Accounting.

What if my data is confidential?

Enterprise engagements include an air-gapped on-premise deployment option. All consulting engagements include NDA by default. ER API will process uploads in memory and delete them within 24 hours — see /privacy for details.
